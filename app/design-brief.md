# Tinny Tummies - design brief

Design read: a calm, trustworthy baby food store for busy Maldivian moms, warm and tender but tidy enough to shop one-handed while holding a baby.
Concept spine: "the family table" - every section is a place setting; soft bowls (rounded arch frames), spoons (pill CTAs), and a daily rhythm (first tastes, snack time, toddler meals).
Delivery tier: editorial (clean shop first, gentle micro-motion).
Animation mode: non-animated - user picked "Lighter motion" at intake.
Motion: mount rise on headlines, floating hero chips, looping muted hero film (Higgsfield), marquee of promises, transform-only scroll reveals, card hover lifts, cart bump. All reduced-motion gated.

Locked palette (fresh direction, user asked for a new brand):
- Milk #FCFBF8 (page), Ink #1E2B26 (text), Leaf #3F6B55 (primary action), Leaf deep #2C5040
- Tints per category: Peach #FFE3D3 (porridge), Butter #FFF1C8 (oats), Sage #DCEADB (snacks), Blush #F8DCE0 (pasta), Lilac #E8E2F6 (pouches)
- Coral #EE6F57 (badges, cart count only)
Defense: food-tint pastels read as fresh produce, the deep leaf green signals natural and safe, near-white keeps it clinical-clean for moms.

Locked type: Bricolage Grotesque (display, 600-800, friendly optical warmth) + Figtree (UI/body, highly legible at small sizes on phones).

Section plan (home): announcement bar, header, split hero with arch video frame, promise marquee, shop by age (round tiles), categories (tinted cards), mom favourites (product grid), brands (typographic list), snack time (image split), how ordering works (numbered steps + image), what we care about (flat-lay band), Instagram CTA, footer.

Asset plan (Higgsfield): hero mom-and-baby film + poster, snack-time, on-the-go pouch, flat-lay, toddler pasta, mother portrait, 5 category sample packshots, OG cover. Real product photos uploadable per product from /admin.

CTA inventory: hero "Shop all" leaf pill with sliding arrow; "Shop by age" underline link; product quick-add round + button with pop; product page "Add to cart" full-width leaf bar with count; "Checkout" coral-edged drawer bar; "Place order" large leaf block; WhatsApp and Viber brand-tint buttons.
